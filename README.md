# Privacy Policy for Legacy at Jordan Lake Golf Score App

**Last Updated:** November 23, 2025

## Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the Legacy at Jordan Lake Golf Score App ("the App") collects, uses, and protects your information. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to being transparent about our data practices.

## Location Services

### What We Collect
The App uses location services to provide you with distance calculations to golf holes during your game.

### How We Use Location Data
Location data is used **solely** for the following purpose:
- **Distance Calculation**: To calculate and display the distance from your current position to golf holes on the course.

### What We DO NOT Do With Your Location Data
We want to be absolutely clear about what we do not do with your location information:
- ❌ **We DO NOT store your location data** on our servers or in any database
- ❌ **We DO NOT share, sell, or disseminate your location data** to any third parties
- ❌ **We DO NOT track your movements** outside of real-time distance calculations
- ❌ **We DO NOT create location histories or profiles**

Your location data is processed locally on your device in real-time and is immediately discarded after the distance calculation is complete.

## Data Collection Summary

| Data Type | Collected | Purpose | Stored | Shared |
|-----------|-----------|---------|--------|--------|
| Location Data | Yes (only when in use) | Distance calculation to holes | No | No |

## Permissions

The App requires location permission to function properly. You can:
- Grant location access when using the App
- Revoke location access at any time through your device settings
- The App will not function without location access, as this is essential for distance calculations
